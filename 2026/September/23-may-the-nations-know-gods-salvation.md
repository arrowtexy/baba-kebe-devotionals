# May the Nations Know God's Salvation

**Wednesday, September 23, 2026**

**Psalm 67**

## Key Verse

> "So that your ways may be known on earth, your salvation among all nations." - Psalm 67:2

## Reflection

God's blessings are never meant to stop with us. When God favours you, it is a sign - He wants the world around you to see Him clearly. Think about it: your healed body, your restored family, your new job - these are not just personal wins. They are God's billboard to your neighbours, your office, your street. You are walking proof that God saves.

## Lessons for Believers

1. God blesses you to attract others to Him.
2. Your testimony carries power beyond your circle.
3. Live visibly so the world sees God's salvation.

## Prayer

Lord, make my life a witness to Your salvation.

## Let's Talk

How do you show God's goodness to those around you?
